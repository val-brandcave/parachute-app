One credit pays for one review type on one appraisal. Buy them in packs, share them across your team, and see exactly where each one went.

What most commercial loans need.

When you reach zero

Reviews already running finish as normal. New reviews wait until someone adds credits. Admins get an email when 2 credits are left.

When credits expire

Purchased credits last 12 months from the day you buy them. Trial credits don’t expire. We always use the oldest credits first.

Questions

When is a credit used?

When a review starts. If a review fails to run on our side, the credit goes back to your balance.
