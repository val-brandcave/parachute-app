Onboarding layout: centred vs anchored

Update (11 Nov): Val picked C: B's content (Book a call in the same card, a side panel that changes per step) with A's centring. In the build, motion absorbs the height jumps (see onboarding-motion.html).

Same four onboarding screens, drawn three ways. A is board 1 as drawn: the card is centred, so its top moves with its height, and Book a call uses its own split layout. B anchors every card 10% down (the lowest that still fits the tallest step on a laptop) the screen, stretches the side panel to the card's height, puts Book a call in the same card, and changes the side panel's content on each step. C keeps B's content and A's centring.
