### Behaviour

- Steps use `router.push`, so browser Back walks the steps. Picking an amount uses `router.replace`, so it is not a step.
- When Pay succeeds, `router.replace(…step=done&receipt=<id>)` runs, so Back can't land on Review (the flow checks this).
- "Exit checkout" calls `router.push(returnHref)`, never `router.back()`.
- **RF4 (coordinator note):** with `types=` in the query, Exit and "Back to your review" go to `returnTo` with `types=<a,b>` merged into its existing query, via `withTypes()` in the hook. Example: `return=/reviews/abc&types=technical` → `/reviews/abc?types=technical`. The flow checks this with a billing return: the URL becomes `…?tab=usage&types=technical`.
- Saved card: Amount → Review. Its CTA reads "Continue to review". On Review the stepper shows Payment as done and lets you click it.
- Pay without the terms shows the inline "Agree to the credit terms to pay" (the terms box turns red) and focuses the checkbox. Pay with `error=card` sends you to Payment, where the decline callout explains why.
- Hard reload: amount, step, return and types are in the URL. Paid reads the `receipt=` entry back from the adapter-backed ledger. `step=done` without a matching receipt shows Amount.
- Reviewer or approver, pending org, invoiced org: the page shows a plain inline explanation ("Only an admin can buy credits" / "Credits open after your setup call" / "Your organization is billed monthly"). For role and approval it also calls `useLockStore.openLock(...)` once on arrival. Pay calls it again for the approval case.
- No `button[disabled]` anywhere. No illustration on Amount, Payment or Review (the flow checks all three); Paid has one scene.

## DS TaskFlow outcome

`TaskFlow presentation="page"` is used with `header`, `aside`, `contentMaxWidth={false}`, no `rail` and no `actions`.

- It works for the frame: the body and the aside scroll independently, so the summary stays put while the form scrolls. The flow scrolls the body in a 560 px-tall window and checks that the summary's top doesn't move.
- The content column fills the rest of the width.
- Its aside is a fixed **340 px**, not 336.
- It has no responsive behaviour. Under 900 px, `checkout.css` stacks the body and the summary into one scroll by targeting TaskFlow's inner wrappers (`.co-flow > div > div`). That depends on TaskFlow's DOM structure.
- DS-candidate: give TaskFlow a responsive `aside` (stack below a breakpoint) and an aside width prop.
- The footer slot (`actions`/`gate`) is unused: the boards put Back / Continue under the form and the main CTA in the summary.

## Deviations

1. **Paid primary label depends on where `return` points.** A `/reviews/…` return → "Back to your review" (spec copy). A `/configure/billing…` return → "Back to billing". Anything else → "Back to where you were". Reason: `CheckoutLauncher` always sets `return` (the current page), so the literal label would read "Back to your review" on a billing round trip.
2. **Paid cards:** the primary (the way back, or Start a review when there is no return), then View receipt, then Set up auto-reload only if it was skipped. With a non-review return and auto-reload already on, Start a review takes the third slot.
3. **Saved-card row drops "Expires 08/28".** `WalletSettings` has no card expiry, so the row reads "Visa ending in 4242 · Dana Reyes". See Requests.
4. **Summary adds "Saving vs. $150 a credit"** from board 2, on top of the §5.6 summary rows.
5. **The Payment form draft (contact, PO, new-card choice, auto-reload opt-in) lives in `sessionStorage`** (`parachute:checkout-form`; card digits are never stored). It is seeded from the wallet settings, so a reload on Review keeps edits that haven't been paid yet. Everything else is in the URL or the wallet.
6. **New card validation:** Continue with fewer than 12 digits shows "Enter the card number" and focuses the field. Expiry and CVC are not checked (prototype).
7. **"Start a review" on Paid goes to `/launchpad`**, the same as the command palette.
8. **Narrow (≤ 640 px):** the top-bar logo hides, and only the current step's label shows (the others show only their numbers). Without this the top bar overlapped at 390.
