## Before → after

**`/welcome` org setup**
- Before: a 560 px card with no panel and no progress bar. The volume options were Under 50 / 50–200 / 200+. Sales answers showed an optional "Book a call" box. Self-serve showed the "free sample review" reassurance. Submit was `disabled={!canContinue}`. `?preset=large` and `?step=pending` were demo hacks. Every submit created a new `org-xxxx`.
- After: one centred 520 px card under a 2-segment `ProgressBar` ("Step 1 of 2"), with the side panel beside it ("What Parachute checks", scene 23; row 2 switches to "review guidelines" for appraisal firms).
  - Volume options come from `VOLUME_BAND_LABEL`: Under 50 / 50–199 / 200+ / I'm not sure yet.
  - Routing is live (`routeForProfile`). At 11+ or 200+, the info callout "Teams your size start with a short call" reveals (height, then opacity + 8 px), the triggering answer gets a petrol dot, and the button cross-fades to "Book a call" with a calendar icon sliding in. Moving back under the threshold reverses all of it. None of this submits.
  - "I'm not sure yet" stays self-serve and shows "We'll offer a call later if you need one."
  - The sample line is gone, and so is `disabled`. An empty name shows an inline error ("Enter your organization's name", `aria-invalid` + `aria-describedby`), shakes the field ±6 px (full motion only) and focuses it.
  - The submit is `Button busy` with a 600 ms mock beat. It upserts `org-hcb` (`upsertStageOrg`, PC-19; approval gated for enterprise), signs in the seeded admin `user-dr` (recreated if a reset removed it), loads session data and grants the trial for self-serve only. It then calls `useStepNav().go("/welcome/invite" | "/welcome/call", 1)`.
- `?preset` and `?step` are dropped.

**Frame (`OnboardingFrame`)**
- The card height is measured: a `ResizeObserver` watches the inner box, and framer `animate()` tweens the card from its real current height to the new one (`cardHeight`). Because the stage is flex-centred, the card and the panel glide together. `overflow: hidden` is applied only while gliding.
- The panel content comes from `usePathname()` and cross-fades with `AnimatePresence mode="popLayout"` (opacity + 8 px).
- Progress: segment 2 fills (scaleX, 400 ms) on the forward step; segment 1 fills during T2.
- Exit before push: `go()` sets `exiting`, the template plays `exit`, then it pushes (400 ms safety timeout). Browser back and forward get enter only, from the correct side: the direction is derived from the step index during render.
- T2 entry: if `takeHandoff()` returns `{from:"verify"}`, the first frame is the card, empty, at the verify rect (fixed). Two frames later a FLIP runs: one `animate(0→1, cardMove)` drives the card's translate and scale **and** the inner box's inverse scale, so text never stretches. The panel and the content join 120 ms in (x +24 / step variants). A safety timer guarantees the frame lands.
- T4: `finish(path, work)` swaps the steps (opacity 0, scale 0.98) for the interstitial (opacity, y 12) with `AnimatePresence mode="wait"`. If `work` rejects, the frame returns to the step with a danger callout in the card.
- `?view=interstitial&path=self_serve|enterprise|enterprise_later` (prototype) is read with `useSyncExternalStore`, so it causes no hydration mismatch. It loops and never navigates.
- Motion tokens reach the CSS as `--onb-*` custom properties built from `lib/motion.ts`. Nothing in `onboarding.css` types its own easing or duration.

**`SetupInterstitial`**
- The copy follows motion-spec §5: self-serve, enterprise booked (the slot comes from `org.profile.callSlot`; the title uses the short name "Getting Harbor ready for your call") and enterprise later (two lines).
- Timing: lines tick at 500, 1000 and 1500 ms. The last line also waits for `work`. The hold after it is 300 ms, and the screen never stays longer than 2.4 s. Under reduced motion or motion off, it shows the finished list for 600 ms.
- On the way out it calls `markArrival({path, at})`, fades (280 ms `ease.exit`) and calls `router.push("/get-started")`.
- Accessibility: the list has `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"`, each finished line gets a visually hidden ", done", focus moves to the `h1`, and the bar is `aria-hidden`.

**Invite and call steps (panel + routing only; the content is T1.2's and T1.3's)**
- Invite: spot `15-request-sent` (128 px, panel on), "What your team gets" with the org name. `onDone`, sent or skipped, starts the interstitial `self_serve`. The domain passed to `InviteStep` comes from the signed-in admin's e-mail.
- Call: scene 25 with "What happens next" (numbered; uses the short name and `TRIAL_GRANT_CREDITS`). When `org.profile.callBookedAt` is set (the card is showing its booked scene), the panel drops its illustration. `onBooked` starts `enterprise_booked`; `onLater` starts `enterprise_later`.
- At ≤ 960 px the panel stacks under the card.

## Deviations

- The org setup panel art is `23-scene-first-review` at 300 px. Spec §5.1 names none; the option C board draws this scene.
- The card's T2 move uses a manual FLIP (framer `animate` driving transform), not framer `layout`. framer's `layout` never animated the fixed → in-flow change in this tree, so the card snapped.
- The card height is animated imperatively (`animate(el, {height:[from,to]})`) rather than with the `animate` prop. With the prop, framer never pinned the height at rest, so step changes snapped.
- Focus after a step change goes to the step `h1`, **unless** the step has already focused something itself. `InviteStep` focuses its first email, as the T1.2 spec requires.
- The interstitial `work` is `loadSessionData`, so Get started lands with fresh stores. The org upsert and the trial grant run on the org-setup submit, and invites and the booking run inside their own steps. That makes it a reload, not the whole job.
- `SetupInterstitial` gained the optional props `loop` and `onFail`, and `StepPanel` now takes `{ step, kind?, orgName, booked? }` (widened from `step: string`).
- The §10 grep for `cubic-bezier` matches two lines in `OnboardingFrame.tsx`. Both are generated from `ease.*` tokens for the CSS custom properties, not typed values.
