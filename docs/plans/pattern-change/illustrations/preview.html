Spot illustrations

Three candidate directions for the onboarding, credits and billing surfaces, then the recommended direction, Drafted, drawn as 12 original spots. Every spot is one inline SVG: the ink is currentColor, and every fill is a CSS variable, so one file covers light and dark.

Three directions

The same two subjects drawn in each direction, on light and dark tiles.

A · Ink + one accent

This is the closest to Apollo: a heavy near-black ink, a solid black offset shadow and one fill colour.

  • It's the most recognisable, and it's the style the design lead praised.
  • It reads loud next to our calm UI. In dark mode it has to sit on its own coloured tile, as Apollo's do.
  • We dropped Apollo's warm yellow accent because amber is already our flag state colour.
  • The risk is that it reads as an Apollo copy, which trades one "borrowed" look for another.

B · Wireframe vignette

This is the Equisant approach: abstracted pieces of our own UI in neutral bars, with a petrol accent.

  • It's the quietest, it's on brand, and it can't look cartoony.
  • It can't show objects. Credits, the Trust Center, a signed workbook and a gift all collapse into "grey bars".
  • At 48 px it turns to mush, and in empty states it looks like a loading skeleton.

C · Drafted Recommended

This is a hybrid. It takes Apollo's object line art (an outline, one accent and a hard offset shadow) and builds it from our own objects.

  • The ink is navy, and the shadow is a navy tint rather than black. That keeps the "designed sticker" depth while staying calm.
  • The objects are domain-true: appraisal pages with a folded corner, cited lines, a signed sheet and an intake tray.
  • The ink flips to light in dark mode, so the spots need no special tile.

Drafted · the rules

Canvas120 × 90 viewBox. The ink stroke is 2.25 (0.9 px at 48 px wide). Detail lines are 1.75 in the muted colour.
One accentExactly one petrol object per spot. The petrol tint is allowed as a second tone of the same hue. No state colours.
Hard shadowOnly the hero object casts a shadow: a solid +3/+3 offset in --ill-shadow, with no blur.
Precision marksInstead of Apollo's sparkles, use at most one small "+" registration mark in the muted colour.
No textGlyphs stand in for labels (a page icon in the citation chip), so the spots scale and localise.
ThemingThe ink is currentColor. The other knobs are --ill-paper, --ill-accent, --ill-tint, --ill-shadow, --ill-muted and --ill-stroke.

Tier 2: scenes

Six larger illustrations for hero places, drawn at 320 × 200 in the same ink. Each one sits on its own tint panel and tells a small story, with at most two petrol objects. One object always breaks the panel edge, so the scene reads as an object on a mat rather than a picture in a box.

The originals stay as they are. Each 01b–12b revision has a larger petrol object, a +4/+4 shadow in a stronger tint (--ill-shadow-strong), and an optional oval panel that switches on when --ill-panel is set. Each card shows the original, the revision, and the revision with its panel on light and dark.
